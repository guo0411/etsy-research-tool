Etsy Digital Research Tool

A private market research tool built with n8n to explore digital product opportunities.

What it does

  • Researches publicly available Etsy listings
  • Compares digital product prices and features
  • Identifies potential market opportunities
  • Organizes historical research results

API Usage

The tool uses read-only access to public listing information. It does not access private buyer data, manage shops, or automatically publish listings.

This tool is intended for personal research use and is not offered as a public service.
